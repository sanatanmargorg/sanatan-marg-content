The 2027 Sankranti dates in India run from Makar Sankranti on 15 January to Dhanu Sankranti on 16 December. The full list below gives the expected Indian calendar date for each of the 12 solar transitions; the exact transition time and observance window depend on your city.

## What are the Sankranti dates in 2027?

Sankranti marks the Sun’s movement from one zodiac sign, or rashi, into the next. The dates below are the main India calendar dates for all 12 Sankrantis in 2027.

| Date in 2027 | Sankranti | Sun enters | Common association |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15 January | Makar Sankranti | Makara, or Capricorn | Harvest, charity and Surya worship |
| 13 February | Kumbha Sankranti | Kumbha, or Aquarius | Solar month change |
| 15 March | Meena Sankranti | Meena, or Pisces | Solar month change |
| 14 April | Mesha Sankranti | Mesha, or Aries | New Year customs in several regions |
| 15 May | Vrishabha Sankranti | Vrishabha, or Taurus | Solar month change |
| 15 June | Mithuna Sankranti | Mithuna, or Gemini | Solar month change |
| 16 July | Karka Sankranti | Karka, or Cancer | Dakshinayana period begins in many traditions |
| 17 August | Simha Sankranti | Simha, or Leo | Solar month change |
| 17 September | Kanya Sankranti | Kanya, or Virgo | Solar month change |
| 17 October | Tula Sankranti | Tula, or Libra | Solar month change |
| 16 November | Vrishchika Sankranti | Vrishchika, or Scorpio | Solar month change |
| 16 December | Dhanu Sankranti | Dhanu, or Sagittarius | Dhanur month begins in some traditions |

Names and customs can vary by region. For example, Mesha Sankranti is linked with several regional New Year celebrations, while Makar Sankranti is widely known as a harvest festival. The dates in the table are useful for planning, but a local panchang should be used for the exact time.

## Why can the date or puja time change by location?

The Sankranti itself is a precise solar event, but the clock time depends on your location and time zone. A date shown for India may not be the same date in the United States, the United Kingdom, Australia or another country.

The observance rule can also affect the festival date. If the Sun enters a sign late in the day, a panchang may place bathing, charity or other religious acts on the same day or the following day, depending on local sunrise and the tradition being followed.

For an exact result, check these details:

1. Your city or nearest major city.
2. The local sunrise and sunset times.
3. The Sankranti entry time, also called Sankranti moment.
4. The recommended punya kala or observance period.
5. Your family or temple tradition if it follows a particular regional calendar.

Do not treat a general date list as a muhurta timetable. It tells you the festival date, not always the best hour for a bath, donation, puja or temple visit.

## How is Sankranti different from a tithi calendar?

Sankranti is based on the Sun’s movement into a zodiac sign. A tithi is based on the changing angular relationship between the Sun and Moon. This is why Sankranti dates and tithi dates should not be used in the same way.

A tithi calendar is used for many important Hindu observances, including Ekadashi, Amavasya, Purnima, Janmashtami and several vrat days. The tithi can begin or end at any time of day, so the date shown on a wall calendar may not tell the whole story.

Purnima 2026, for example, belongs to a lunar calendar for an earlier year. It should not be mixed with the 2027 Sankranti list. If you are planning a vrat or puja, check the correct year, city and tithi before setting a date.

## How should you observe Sankranti at home?

A simple Sankranti observance can include a clean bath, prayer to Surya Dev, offering water to the rising Sun and a donation made with respect. You do not need an elaborate setup. Sincere prayer, self-discipline and kindness are central to many household practices.

### A simple morning plan

- Wake up early and bathe before beginning the puja.
- Wear clean clothes and prepare a small space for prayer.
- Offer water to the Sun while standing in a safe, open place.
- Recite a prayer, Surya mantra or a name of your chosen deity.
- Give food, clothes, sesame, jaggery or another useful item to someone in need, according to your means.
- Share a simple meal with family and avoid waste.

Sesame, jaggery, rice, khichdi and seasonal foods are linked with Sankranti in different parts of India. Customs are not identical everywhere. Follow the practice of your home, community or temple without assuming that one regional custom applies to all Hindus.

Makar Sankranti often receives the most attention because it is linked with harvest celebrations, kite flying in some regions, charitable giving and worship of Surya. If you plan a fast, donation or temple visit, confirm the local punya kala first.

## Which 2027 Sankranti dates need extra planning?

Makar Sankranti on 15 January is the date most families mark in their annual calendar. It may be connected with regional celebrations such as Pongal, Uttarayan or Magh Bihu, although each festival has its own customs and local name.

Mesha Sankranti on 14 April also matters in several parts of India. It is connected with regional New Year observances, harvest traditions and temple events. Karka Sankranti on 16 July and Dhanu Sankranti on 16 December may also be important for people who follow solar-month observances or particular temple calendars.

If you are arranging travel, a community meal or a large puja, check the local panchang several days in advance. Festival programmes may use the date of the main public observance rather than the exact solar entry time.

## How do related Hindu calendar questions fit together?

Many calendar questions look similar but refer to different systems or practices. Keeping the difference clear prevents missed vrat days and wrong puja timings.

### What does Om Namo Narayanaya mean?

“Om Namo Narayanaya” is a well-known Vaishnava mantra. It means “I bow to Narayana” or “Salutations to Narayana,” with Narayana understood as a name of the Divine, commonly associated with Vishnu. It can be recited as part of personal prayer, but it does not determine a Sankranti date.

### When is Chhath Puja?

If you ask, “Chhath Puja kab hai?”, you are asking about a lunar festival, not a monthly solar transition. Chhath Puja is usually observed after Diwali on Kartika Shukla Shashthi, with offerings to Surya and prayers for family well-being. Use a local tithi calendar for its exact date and sunrise or sunset timings.

### What is Jai Ambe Gauri?

Jai Ambe Gauri is a popular aarti dedicated to Devi. If you want the lyrics and meaning for home worship, read this [Jai Ambe Gauri Aarti guide](https://sanatanmarg.org/blog/jai-ambe-gauri-aarti-hindi-meaning). The aarti can be part of daily or festival prayer, while Sankranti dates come from the solar calendar.
