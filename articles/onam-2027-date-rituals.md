## When Is Onam 2027, And What Are the Ten Days?

Onam 2027 falls on Sunday, 12 September in India. The main day is Thiruvonam, the tenth day of Kerala's harvest festival; the 10-day observance begins with Atham on 3 September.

Onam follows the Malayalam calendar, so the exact dates change each year. The dates below show the traditional sequence for 2027. Spellings may differ between calendars and regions.

| Onam day | Date in 2027 | Traditional focus |
|---|---|---|
| Atham | 3 September | Festival begins; pookalam starts |
| Chithira | 4 September | Pookalam grows larger |
| Chodhi | 5 September | Home cleaning and preparations |
| Vishakam | 6 September | Food and festival planning |
| Anizham | 7 September | Community celebrations |
| Thriketta | 8 September | Family visits and offerings |
| Moolam | 9 September | Temple and cultural events |
| Pooradam | 10 September | Clay figures may be prepared |
| Uthradam | 11 September | Final preparations |
| Thiruvonam | 12 September | Main day of Onam |

## Why Is Onam Celebrated?

Onam is a harvest festival linked with Kerala, gratitude, family gatherings, hospitality, and the traditional story of King Mahabali. Many people believe that Mahabali returns to visit his people during this time.

The story also includes Vamana, an avatar of Lord Vishnu. Vamana asks Mahabali for as much land as he can cover in three steps. The tale is understood in different ways, but it is widely connected with humility, devotion, generosity, and the care of a good ruler.

Onam is not limited to one kind of celebration. Some families focus on prayer, while others give more time to the sadya, pookalam, cultural events, or visits with relatives. Sharing food and kindness is central to the spirit of the festival.

## How Can You Celebrate Onam at Home?

You can celebrate Onam at home with a clean space, a simple flower design, prayer, festive food, and time with family. A large feast or an elaborate decoration is not required.

A simple home celebration can include:

1. Clean the entrance and prayer space.
2. Make a small pookalam with fresh flowers or petals.
3. Light a lamp safely and offer flowers, fruit, or a home-cooked dish.
4. Pray to Lord Vishnu, Vamana, or the family deity according to your tradition.
5. Prepare a simple vegetarian meal or one special dish such as payasam.
6. Share food with family, neighbours, guests, or someone who needs help.
7. Wear clean or traditional clothes and spend a few quiet minutes in gratitude.

If you live outside Kerala, you can follow the same spirit with the flowers, ingredients, and customs available to you. The value of the celebration comes from sincerity, sharing, and remembrance, not from spending a lot of money.

## How Do You Make a Simple Pookalam?

A pookalam is a floral design made near the entrance of a home or another shared space. It usually grows in size and detail during the 10 days of Onam, with the largest design made around Thiruvonam.

### A simple method

1. Sweep and clean a flat area.
2. Mark a circle or another simple outline with rice flour or chalk.
3. Arrange flowers or petals by colour, starting from the centre.
4. Keep the design low and easy to walk around.
5. Remove the flowers respectfully after the festival and compost them where possible.

Use safe, locally available flowers. Avoid blocking a doorway, using slippery materials, or placing lamps where children and pets can knock them over.

## What Is Served At an Onam Sadya?

An Onam sadya is a traditional vegetarian feast. It is often served on a banana leaf, with rice, several vegetable preparations, pickles, chutneys, and payasam. The exact menu differs by family, community, and region.

Common parts of a sadya may include:

- Rice as the main meal
- Avial or other mixed vegetable dishes
- Sambar, rasam, and a lentil preparation
- Thoran, olan, kalan, or pachadi
- Pickles, banana chips, and papad
- One or more varieties of payasam

You do not need to cook every dish. Choose a few foods your family enjoys, label ingredients if guests have allergies, and serve only what can be eaten. Eating together and sharing with others matter more than the size of the menu.

## Which Prayers and Mantras Fit an Onam Celebration?

A Vishnu-focused prayer is a natural choice for Onam. The meaning of Om Namo Narayanaya is a salutation to Lord Narayana, a name of Vishnu. You may chant it quietly, recite it a few times, or use it as part of a longer prayer according to your family practice.

### Other family prayers

Om Shree Hanumate Namah means salutations to Lord Hanuman. It is not an Onam-specific mantra, but families may include it if Hanuman prayer is part of their daily worship.

Jai Shiv Omkara is a Shiva aarti, and Jaya Ambe Gauri is a Devi aarti. Neither is required for Onam, yet either may be included in a home prayer routine when it reflects the family's tradition. Keep the prayer simple and respectful. You do not need to combine every mantra or aarti in one sitting.

## How Can You Check the Right Puja Time?

The festival date is the same across India, but the best time for a specific puja can vary by city. It may depend on local sunrise, the Thiruvonam nakshatra period, and your family tradition.

For a careful plan:

- Check a panchang set for your city.
- Confirm the local Thiruvonam nakshatra period.
- Ask your family priest if your home follows a particular puja method.
- Do not copy a timing from Kerala if you are in another part of India or another country.
- If no special timing is available, pray during a calm part of the day when the family can gather.

The main date is easy to remember, but a city-specific panchang is better for exact timings.

## What Mistakes Should You Avoid During Onam?

The most common mistake is confusing the first day with the main day. Atham begins the 10-day observance, while Thiruvonam on 12 September 2027 is the central day.

Also avoid treating one regional custom as the only correct form of Onam. Families may differ in their food, prayer, pookalam design, and way of telling the Mahabali story. Other useful points are:

- Do not copy an auspicious time without checking your location.
- Do not feel that a costly feast or large pookalam is necessary.
- Avoid food waste, unsafe lamps, and flowers treated with harmful chemicals.
- Be clear about ingredients when serving guests.
- Leave room for quiet prayer, not only decoration and social events.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Onam on 12 September 2027?

Yes. Thiruvonam, the main day of Onam, falls on Sunday, 12 September 2027 in India.

### When does the Onam celebration begin in 2027?

The traditional 10-day observance begins with Atham on Friday, 3 September 2027. Thiruvonam is on 12 September.

### What is the main ritual of Onam?

There is no single required ritual for every family. Common practices include making a pookalam, offering prayer, sharing an Onam sadya, and remembering Mahabali and Vishnu.

### Can I celebrate Onam outside Kerala?

Yes. Families anywhere can mark Onam with a clean prayer space, a flower design, simple food, prayer, and acts of sharing. Use your local panchang if you want the exact nakshatra period.
