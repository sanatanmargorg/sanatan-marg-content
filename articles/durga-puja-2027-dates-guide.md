Durga Puja 2027 will be observed mainly from Wednesday, 6 October, to Sunday, 10 October, in the Bengali calendar, with Mahalaya expected on Thursday, 30 September. Maha Saptami is 7 October, Maha Ashtami is 8 October, Maha Navami is 9 October, and Vijaya Dashami is 10 October. Exact tithi timings can differ by city, so check a local panjika before fixing your puja time.

## What Are the Durga Puja 2027 Dates?

For the commonly followed Bengali calendar, the main Durga Puja days run from Maha Shashthi to Vijaya Dashami. Navratri begins around 1 October, but the five-day Durga Puja celebration begins later with Shashthi. Mahalaya marks the start of Devi Paksha and is a separate observance before the main puja days.

| Day | Date in 2027 | Main observance |
|---|---|---|
| Mahalaya | 30 September, Thursday | Devi Paksha begins; many families offer prayers for ancestors |
| Maha Shashthi | 6 October, Wednesday | Bodhan and the formal beginning of Durga Puja in many traditions |
| Maha Saptami | 7 October, Thursday | Saptami puja and invocation of the Goddess |
| Maha Ashtami | 8 October, Friday | Main worship, pushpanjali and, in some places, Kumari Puja |
| Maha Navami | 9 October, Saturday | Navami puja and completion of the main worship |
| Vijaya Dashami | 10 October, Sunday | Farewell, sindoor traditions in some communities and immersion |

These dates are a useful planning guide, not a replacement for your local calendar. A tithi can begin or end at a different clock time in different places. This matters most for pushpanjali, Sandhi Puja, fasting and immersion.

## What Do Mahalaya and the Five Main Days Mean?

Mahalaya is a quiet transition into Devi Paksha. Many people listen to devotional recitations, remember departed family members and prepare for the arrival of Maa Durga. Customs vary by family and region, so there is no need to copy one practice if it is not part of your tradition.

### Maha Shashthi

Shashthi is linked with the formal welcome of the Goddess. In many Bengali traditions, Bodhan is performed to awaken or invoke Durga before the main days of worship. Public pandals often open their celebrations around this time.

### Maha Saptami

Saptami marks the next full day of worship. Families and community organisers prepare offerings, flowers, food and the image of the Goddess. The details of the ritual can differ between homes, temples and public pujas.

### Maha Ashtami

Ashtami is often the busiest and most deeply observed day. Devotees may offer pushpanjali, keep a fast or attend a special puja. Sandhi Puja takes place at the junction of Ashtami and Navami, so use a local panchang for its exact timing rather than relying on a general clock time.

### Maha Navami and Dashami

Navami completes the main worship. Dashami is a day of farewell and blessings. In some regions, women take part in sindoor customs, families exchange greetings, and idols are taken for immersion. Not every community follows the same Dashami practice.

## How Can You Check the Exact Puja Timings?

Use the calendar for your own city and confirm the tithi before planning a fast, pushpanjali or Sandhi Puja. The date printed on a festival calendar is helpful, but the start and end of a tithi may fall at different times in Kolkata, Delhi, Mumbai, London or another location.

Follow these simple checks:

1. Select your city or nearest location in a reliable Hindu calendar.
2. Confirm the local sunrise and the tithi for each puja day.
3. Check separate timings for Bodhan, pushpanjali, Sandhi Puja and immersion.
4. Ask your family priest or temple if your household follows a special custom.
5. Keep some time free for traffic, queues and changes at a public pandal.

A common mistake is to treat the whole civil date as the full tithi. Another is to mix Sharadiya Navratri dates with the five Durga Puja days. They are closely linked, but they are not always described in the same way.

## How Can You Perform a Simple Durga Puja at Home?

You can worship Maa Durga at home with a clean space, a sincere prayer and offerings that fit your family tradition. A large setup is not required. If you already follow instructions from your household priest, keep those instructions first.

### Prepare the space

Clean a small table or puja area. Place a picture or murti of Maa Durga on a clean cloth. Keep flowers, water, a lamp, incense if suitable for your home, fruits or another simple offering, and a bell if you use one.

### Follow a simple order

- Bathe or wash your hands and feet before worship.
- Light the lamp and sit quietly for a moment.
- State your prayer or sankalp in simple words.
- Offer water, flowers, food and a respectful prayer.
- Recite a Durga mantra, read a short stotra or sing an aarti.
- End with gratitude and share the prasad with family.

Do not begin a complex ritual from a random list without understanding its steps. A simple, steady puja is better than a rushed ceremony done only to follow a checklist.

## How Should You Plan a Pandal Visit or Immersion?

Plan public celebrations with respect for the organisers, residents and environment. Check opening hours and local arrangements before leaving, especially on Ashtami and Dashami when crowds are often larger.

Keep these points in mind:

- Carry water and allow extra travel time.
- Follow queues and do not block entrances or paths.
- Ask before taking photographs of private rituals or devotees.
- Keep children close in crowded areas.
- Use flowers and offerings only where organisers allow them.
- Follow local rules for idol immersion and avoid putting non-biodegradable items in water.

Public Durga Puja is both worship and community life. The pandal, music, food and art can be joyful, but the devotional purpose should remain at the centre.

## How Can You Track Other Hindu Festival Dates?

A yearly Hindu calendar becomes easier to use when you know which calculation each festival follows. Ekadashi dates depend on the lunar tithi and local sunrise. Gudi Padwa is based on Chaitra Shukla Pratipada. Sankranti festivals follow the Sun's movement into a zodiac sign, so their observance is calculated differently from a lunar festival.

The next Amavasya and a Purnima in November 2026 also need local tithi details. The moon phase may be visible for a broad area, but the religious date and fasting rule can depend on when the tithi touches sunrise or another required period. Check the city, date and purpose together rather than using one fixed calendar for every place.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When is Durga Puja 2027?

The main five days are expected from 6 to 10 October 2027. Mahalaya is expected on 30 September, and local tithi timings should be checked for your city.

### Is Navratri the same as Durga Puja?

They are related but not identical in every calendar. Sharadiya Navratri covers nine nights of worship, while the main Bengali Durga Puja observance usually refers to Shashthi through Dashami.

### What should I do if my local calendar shows different dates?

Follow the calendar prepared for your city and tradition. Differences usually come from location, sunrise and the exact start or end of a tithi, so ask a local temple or priest if the change affects an important ritual.

### Can I perform Durga Puja at home?

Yes. You can keep a simple home puja with a clean space, a picture or murti, a lamp, flowers, food and sincere prayer. Follow your family tradition for any detailed ritual or fasting rule.
