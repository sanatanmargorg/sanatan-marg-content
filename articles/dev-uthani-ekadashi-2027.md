In India, Dev Uthani Ekadashi in 2027 falls on Tuesday, 9 November 2027. The fast is kept on that date, while Parana, the ritual fast-breaking, is on Wednesday, 10 November after sunrise and within the correct Dwadashi window. Check a local panchang for the exact time in your city.

## What is the date of Dev Uthani Ekadashi 2027?

Dev Uthani Ekadashi is also called Devuthani Ekadashi, Prabodhini Ekadashi, and Haribodhini Ekadashi. It is the Shukla Paksha Ekadashi of the Hindu month of Kartik. The day is linked with the awakening of Lord Vishnu after the four-month Chaturmas period.

Most Indian calendars place the 2027 observance on Tuesday, 9 November. The date can vary in some regional calendars because Hindu festivals follow the tithi at local sunrise, not only the civil date. This is why the Parana time should be checked for your location rather than copied from a general list.

## What does Dev Uthani Ekadashi mean?

The name Dev Uthani means the awakening of the Lord. According to Hindu tradition, Lord Vishnu rests during Chaturmas and awakens on this Ekadashi. Devotees mark the day with prayer, fasting, Vishnu puja, and a renewed focus on dharma.

The festival also has a place in the household calendar. Many families perform or begin Tulsi Vivah around this time. Some communities consider the day a fresh start for marriages, religious ceremonies, and other auspicious work, although the right muhurta must still be checked separately.

Ekadashi is the eleventh lunar day. Dev Uthani is not just a day for avoiding food. Its purpose is to reduce distraction, control the senses, remember Vishnu, and spend more time in prayer and good conduct.

## How should you observe the Ekadashi fast?

Choose a form of fasting that suits your health, age, work, and family tradition. A strict Nirjala fast, without food or water, is not required for every devotee. Many people observe a water-only, fruit, milk, or vrat-food fast instead.

### On Dashami, the day before

Eat a simple meal and avoid overeating. Many households stop eating grains and beans from Dashami evening, while others begin their food restrictions after sunrise on Ekadashi. Follow the practice taught in your home or sampradaya.

Try to keep the mind calm. Avoid harsh speech, unnecessary arguments, and late-night distractions. The fast is meant to support prayer, not create stress.

### On Ekadashi morning

Wake, bathe, wear clean clothes, and make a simple sankalp for the fast. Clean the puja space and place an image or murti of Lord Vishnu. You may offer water, flowers, Tulsi leaves, a lamp, incense, and fruit according to your practice.

A basic puja can include:

1. A short prayer and sankalp for Ekadashi.
2. Lighting a diya and offering water, flowers, and Tulsi.
3. Chanting a Vishnu mantra or reading a devotional passage.
4. Offering simple sattvic food as naivedya.
5. Doing aarti and praying for strength, wisdom, and devotion.

Many devotees chant Om Namo Narayanaya. The mantra means I bow to Narayana, a name of Lord Vishnu. It can be repeated quietly during puja, while travelling, or whenever you need to bring the mind back to prayer.

### What foods are usually avoided?

Rice, wheat, ordinary grains, and beans are commonly avoided on Ekadashi. Depending on the household, devotees may eat fruits, milk, nuts, potatoes, makhana, or specially prepared vrat food. Rock salt is used in some traditions instead of regular salt.

Rules differ across regions and Vaishnava traditions. Do not judge another devotee's practice by your own household rules. Pregnant people, older adults, children, people with diabetes, and anyone taking regular medicine should choose a safe form of observance and seek medical advice when needed.

## How and when should you break the fast?

Parana should be done on Dwadashi, the day after Ekadashi, after sunrise and during the correct Parana period. For Dev Uthani Ekadashi in India, this is Wednesday, 10 November 2027. The exact window depends on your city because sunrise and tithi end times are not the same everywhere.

Do not break the fast at midnight simply because the calendar date has changed. Do not wait until late in the day without checking the local panchang, since Dwadashi may end before then in some places. Some Vaishnava calendars also observe special rules about waiting until a particular part of Dwadashi has passed.

Begin with water and a light sattvic meal. Fruit, rice-free food, or a simple home-cooked meal may be suitable, depending on your tradition. Avoid overeating after a full-day fast.

### Common mistakes to avoid

- Using a city from another state to copy the Parana time.
- Assuming every Ekadashi has the same fasting rules.
- Starting a strict Nirjala fast without considering your health.
- Breaking the fast at midnight or after the Dwadashi window.
- Treating fasting as the whole observance while forgetting prayer, kindness, and self-control.

## How is Dev Uthani Ekadashi different from Sankranti, Purnima, and Chhath Puja?

Dev Uthani Ekadashi is based on a lunar tithi and falls on the eleventh day of the bright half of Kartik. Sankranti marks the Sun's movement into a zodiac sign, so it follows a solar calculation. There are several Sankrantis in a year, and the name of the specific Sankranti is needed before its 2027 date can be given.

Purnima means the full-moon tithi. A Purnima in 2026 belongs to a different calendar year and does not set the date of Dev Uthani Ekadashi in 2027. Both may be important days for worship, but they follow different tithis.

Chhath Puja is also a separate observance. It is a four-day festival dedicated mainly to Surya Dev and Chhathi Maiya, usually observed soon after Diwali in many parts of India. Its date, sunrise, and sunset timings should be checked in a local calendar rather than combined with the Ekadashi schedule.

## How can you prepare for the day?

Keep the observance simple and steady. Confirm the date and Parana time for your city, plan suitable food, and set aside time for puja or japa. If you work or travel, a short sincere prayer is better than an elaborate plan that leaves you anxious.
