## What Does a Hindu Calendar Show?

A Hindu calendar, often called a panchang, shows tithi, lunar months, festivals, fasting days, nakshatra and other daily details. It helps you choose the right day for puja, vrat, temple visits and family observances according to your place and tradition.

Unlike a simple wall calendar, a Hindu calendar follows the movement of the Sun and Moon. A tithi may begin or end at any time of day, so festival dates and puja timings can change from one city to another.

## What Are the Five Parts of a Panchang?

A panchang has five main parts. Together, they describe the day and help you understand why a date is suitable for a festival, fast or religious activity.

| Panchang part | What it means | Why it helps |
|---|---|---|
| Tithi | The current lunar day | Used to mark festivals, vrat and religious observances |
| Vara | The weekday | Helps identify the day of the week and daily worship plans |
| Nakshatra | The Moon's position among the lunar mansions | Used in some puja, naming and muhurat traditions |
| Yoga | A daily combination of the Sun's and Moon's positions | Included in detailed panchang readings |
| Karana | Half of a tithi | Used in some muhurat and daily calculations |

### What Is a Tithi?

A tithi is a lunar day. It is based on the changing distance between the Sun and Moon, not on midnight-to-midnight clock time. This is why a tithi can start in the afternoon and end the next morning.

### What Is Nakshatra?

Nakshatra refers to the section of the sky where the Moon is placed at a given time. Many Hindu calendars show the nakshatra along with sunrise, sunset, moonrise and moonset.

## Why Do Hindu Festival Dates Change Each Year?

Hindu festivals follow lunar or lunisolar calculations, so their dates move on the Gregorian calendar. The local sunrise, sunset, moonrise and the exact start or end of a tithi can also affect the date shown for an observance.

Regional customs add another layer. Some calendars use the amanta system, in which the month ends with Amavasya. Others use the purnimanta system, in which the month ends with Purnima. The same festival may still be observed on the same general day, but the month name or fasting rule may look different.

For a reliable result, set your city or nearest town before reading a festival date. A calendar set to Delhi may show a different puja time from one set to London, Dubai or New York.

## How Can You Use a Hindu Calendar Each Day?

Start with the daily view, then open the full details for the tithi or event you want to observe. These steps work for most printed and digital calendars:

1. Select your city, time zone and preferred language.
2. Check the sunrise and sunset times for the day.
3. Read the tithi and note when it starts and ends.
4. Look for vrat, festival, parana and puja timings.
5. Follow your family, temple or sampradaya tradition if it uses a special rule.

A daily calendar can also help you plan a simple routine. You may choose a time for prayer, read about the day's deity or festival, prepare for a fast, or check the best period for a puja. Calendar timings are guides; devotion, cleanliness and sincere practice remain central.

## How Can You Check Guru Purnima in 2026 or 2027?

Guru Purnima is observed on Ashadha Purnima in many Hindu calendars. To find its date in 2026 or 2027, select the year and your location, then look for the Purnima observance and its relevant timings.

The date displayed can vary by time zone and by the rule used for the observance. If you plan a fast, satsang, puja or visit to a guru, check the tithi at your location instead of relying only on a date copied from another city.

## How Do You Find the Ugadi 2027 Date?

Ugadi is the New Year festival observed mainly in Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. It is linked with Chaitra Shukla Pratipada, the first tithi of the bright half of the Chaitra month.

For the 2027 Ugadi date, choose 2027 in a location-based Hindu calendar and check the local sunrise and tithi details. Regional calendars may use different names or display related New Year observances separately, so select the tradition that matches your family practice.

## How Do You Find the Next Amavasya?

Amavasya is the new-moon tithi. To find the next Amavasya, open the lunar calendar for your city and check the date, start time and end time of Amavasya. The next occurrence depends on your current date and time zone.

Many families use Amavasya for remembrance of ancestors, simple prayer, charity or other observances. The exact practice differs, so follow the guidance of your family or local priest for rituals such as tarpan or shraddha.

## Where Can You See an Ekadashi List for 2027?

Ekadashi is the eleventh tithi of each lunar fortnight. There are usually two Ekadashis in a lunar month, one during Shukla Paksha and one during Krishna Paksha. A 2027 Ekadashi list should show the date for your city, the fasting tithi and the parana time for breaking the fast.

Do not use a list without checking its location and calculation method. Parana may need to be done after sunrise and within a specific period, and some devotees follow additional rules based on their tradition or health needs. Anyone with a medical condition should take proper medical advice before fasting.

## What Mistakes Should You Avoid With a Hindu Calendar?

A few simple checks prevent most confusion:

- Do not assume that a festival has the same Gregorian date every year.
- Do not copy a puja time from a calendar made for another city.
- Do not confuse a tithi's date with the exact period during which it is active.
- Check whether a fast is based on sunrise, moonrise or another local rule.
- Read the parana time before beginning an Ekadashi fast.
- Respect your family's regional calendar if it differs from a general online listing.

A calendar gives useful calculations, but it cannot replace your family tradition or the advice of a trusted priest for a complex ritual. Use it as a clear guide for planning, then observe with care and understanding.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is a Hindu calendar the same as a panchang?

A Hindu calendar is often presented as a panchang, but the terms are not always used in exactly the same way. A panchang gives detailed daily elements such as tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana and vara, while a calendar may show only dates and festivals.

### Why does a Hindu festival date differ between cities?

Festival dates and timings can change because tithi, sunrise, sunset and moonrise occur at different local times. The calendar should be set to the city where you will observe the festival.

### How can I find the next Amavasya?

Open a location-based Hindu calendar and check the next new-moon tithi, including its start and end times. The result depends on your current date, time zone and selected city.

### What should an Ekadashi list include?

A useful Ekadashi list should include the fasting date, the local tithi details and the parana time. It should also make clear which city, time zone and calendar tradition were used.
